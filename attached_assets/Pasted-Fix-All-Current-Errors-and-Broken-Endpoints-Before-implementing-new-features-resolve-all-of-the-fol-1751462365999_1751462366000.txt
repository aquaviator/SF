2. Shift Status Logic – Required Functionality
A. Status Values
Every shift must always have one of the following statuses visible in all shift lists, calendar, day, and modal views (past, present, future):

Open: Available for anyone to claim (unassigned, visible as available)

Claimed: Claimed by a staff member (but not yet confirmed/accepted by manager or system)

Assigned: Directly assigned to a staff member (awaiting their confirmation, not yet claimed by them; if declined, returns to Open unless owner override)

Confirmed: Assigned/claimed and confirmed/accepted by staff (the person has accepted and committed)

Clocked In: Staff member has clocked in (shift is in progress)

Clocked Out: Staff member has finished and clocked out

Completed: Shift fully completed (could be set automatically after clock out and any post-shift checks)

Declined: Assignment was declined (reverts to Open or gets hidden, based on business rule)

Cancelled: Shift was cancelled by manager/owner

B. Status Transitions (Functional)
Open → Claimed (when a staff member claims)

Open → Assigned (when manager/owner assigns a staff member)

Claimed/Assigned → Confirmed (when staff confirms assignment)

Confirmed → Clocked In (when staff starts work)

Clocked In → Clocked Out

Clocked Out → Completed

Assigned/Claimed → Declined (if staff declines, shift returns to Open unless reassigned by owner)

Any status → Cancelled (if manager cancels at any point)

C. Implementation Requirements
The shift status must be correctly set/updated at every state change, and always displayed in all shift views (calendar, list, modals, day view).

Past, present, and future shifts must all show the correct status.

All forms/modals must display and allow status transitions as appropriate (e.g., claim, assign, confirm, clock in/out, complete, decline, cancel).

If status change fails (e.g., API/network error), show a visible warning and do not update UI until confirmed.
