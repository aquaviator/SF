Here’s a simple, robust way to enforce a seat-based model in ShiftFlo:

1. Key Concepts
Seats Included (seats_included on subscription): how many active staff the tenant is allowed.

Seats Used (live count): number of users in the “Staff” list who are active (not archived/deactivated).

2. On Upgrade
Admin increases seats_included (e.g. from 10 → 15).

Result: seats_used (≤10) is now under the new limit, so the tenant can invite or activate up to 5 more staff without any action.

3. On Downgrade
Admin decreases seats_included (e.g. from 15 → 10).

Immediately compute seats_used (still 15).

Mark subscription “Over Limit” (set a flag or status).

Prevent any new invites or activations until compliance is restored.

Notify the tenant (email + banner in Admin UI):

“You have 15 active staff but only 10 seats. Please archive or deactivate 5 users to restore service.”

Allow manual clean-up:

In the Workforce List, highlight the bottom 5 users (e.g. by join date or alphabetical).

Offer “Archive” or “Deactivate” actions.

As soon as seats_used drops to ≤ seats_included, clear the “Over Limit” flag and re-enable invites.

4. Enforcement Points
Invite/Activation API: always check

js
Copy
Edit
if (seats_used >= seats_included) 
  throw Error("Seat limit reached – upgrade or deactivate staff first");
Daily Compliance Job: send “Over Limit” reminders if still out of compliance 24 hrs after downgrade.

5. UI Feedback
On the Staff list, show a header:

“10 / 10 seats used” (green)
or
“15 / 10 seats used – please deactivate 5 staff” (red banner + button links to archive interface)

Disable the “Add Staff” button when over limit.

6. Data Model
subscriptions: already has seats_included.

Compute seats_used with a simple query:

sql
Copy
Edit
SELECT COUNT(*) FROM users 
WHERE tenant_id = X AND role = 'staff' AND is_active = true;
Optionally add a boolean column over_limit or a status code on subscriptions.

This gives you a secure, transparent seat model where upgrades unlock new seats automatically, and downgrades require a brief clean-up to stay within the new limit—no surprises and no ghost-removals.
