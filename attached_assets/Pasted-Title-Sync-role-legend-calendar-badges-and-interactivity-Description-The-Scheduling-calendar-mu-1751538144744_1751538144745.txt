Title: Sync role legend, calendar badges, and interactivity

Description:
The Scheduling calendar must render exactly the same role initials and colors that are defined in Business Settings → Roles. Currently:

The badge initials on calendar cells (B, C, M, S) don’t match our role names (Bartender, Chef, Manager, Security, Lighting, Supervisor), and they all use the default slate color.

Colors assigned in the CRUD for Roles are not persisting (they reset on page refresh).

On mobile the badge-only mode is fine, but the initials collapse are incorrect and still slate-gray.

Calendar cells aren’t clickable (tapping a shift does nothing).

Acceptance Criteria:

Legend CRUD → Calendar Sync: The “Legend” in Business Settings must drive both the dot colors and badge initials in the calendar. E.g. Lighting → “L” in your custom grey, Bartender → “B” in blue, Security → “S” in yellow, etc.

Persisted Colors: Role color updates in the CRUD must save to Firestore (or your DB) and survive a full page reload.

Responsive Badges: Desktop shows colored squares with initials; mobile shows colored dots only (no text).

Badge Initials Match Role Name: The initial on each badge must be the first letter of the role name you set.

Calendar Cell Clickable: Tapping or clicking any shift cell opens its detail modal (or handler) exactly as before—there should be no dead zones.

Tests:

Visual: After assigning colors to Lighting (gray), Bartender (blue), Security (yellow), Supervisor (purple) in Settings, verify those exact colors and initials appear in Scheduling → Calendar on desktop and mobile.

Persistence: Refresh page and/or restart server—colors and initials remain correct.

Interactivity: Click/tap three different shift cells—each should open its detail view without fail across breakpoints.

Mobile Collapse: Shrink viewport to <640px—badges collapse to dots only, with no initials.

Let me know when this is live so we can confirm the calendar legend and badge behavior are fully aligned and clickable!
