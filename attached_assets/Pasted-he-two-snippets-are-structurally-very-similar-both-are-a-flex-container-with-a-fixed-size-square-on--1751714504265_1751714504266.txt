Renders a <span> with the initials (“SW”) because no <img> ever made it in.

That happens whenever:

userProfile.photoUrl is null or empty,

or our imgErrored flag is true (onError),
so the <img> branch is skipped and the fallback initials are rendered instead.

The outer wrapper has extra padding, background (bg-gray-50), and rounded corners.

tl;dr
BrandedHeader always shows your logo via <img>.

SidebarNav will only show <img> if userProfile.photoUrl is set and loads successfully; otherwise it falls back to a <span> of initials.

That’s why you’re seeing initials in the avatar spot—even though your logo <img> works, your user’s photo never actually made it into the sidebar’s <img> tag. You need to ensure userProfile.photoUrl is populated and not triggering the onError path so that <img src={photoUrl}…/> actually renders instead of the initials.
