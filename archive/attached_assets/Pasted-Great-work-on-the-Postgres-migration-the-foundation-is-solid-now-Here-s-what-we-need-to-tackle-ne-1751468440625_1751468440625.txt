2. Data Not Persisting Across Navigation
Symptom: You click away from Business Settings and upon return, all “saved” changes are gone.

Likely Cause:

Your mutations either aren’t actually committing to Postgres (missing await db.insert…), or you’re not invalidating/refetching the React Query cache after a success.

Action:

Audit each mutation handler on the server: ensure it calls Drizzle’s execute and returns the newly upserted record.

In your React Query onSuccess callbacks, call queryClient.invalidateQueries(['business-profile', tenantId]) (and similarly for operating hours, job roles, locations).
