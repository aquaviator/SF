🔵 Owner Side
Owner Dashboard (/owner/dashboard)

Metrics & Analytics widgets: total shifts, filled vs open, labor cost, upcoming holidays

Quick Actions: buttons for “New Shift,” “Add Staff,” “Create Template”

Staff Status Summary: on-clock vs off-clock, pending assignments

Recent Activity Log: audit trail of key events (shifts created, swaps accepted, etc.)

Tests: smoke-test render + interactions (e.g. clicking “Quick Action” opens correct modal)

Scheduling Section (/owner/scheduling)

Shift Planner: calendar or list view with create/edit/delete via ModalForm

Templates Management: CRUD for shift templates

Live Operations: clock-in/out dashboard showing real-time statuses

Tests: verify that planner loads shifts, modals open, template CRUD works

Workforce Section (/owner/workforce)

Staff Management: full CRUD for staff users

Role Assignments: assign Chef, Bar Staff, Manager roles

Holiday Entitlements: view/set per-staff holiday allowances

Performance Tracking: basic stats (shifts completed, late clock-ins)

Tests: add/edit/delete staff, change role, holiday form validation

Business Settings (/owner/settings)

Business Profile: name, address, branding/logo upload

Manage Roles: create/edit job positions & default permissions

Operating Hours: set open/close times per weekday

Tests: form field renders, save correctly calls storage API

Policies Section (/owner/policies)

Shift Policies: min-notice hours, cancellation rules, strike point system

Notifications: configure email/SMS settings, templates for alerts

Tests: policy form validation, save calls storage

Analytics Section (/owner/analytics)

Reports: generate PDF/CSV for labor cost, shift fill rates

Time & Cost Insights: charts of hours vs spend (can use Recharts)

Activity Logs: paginated audit trail with filters

Tests: render charts (smoke), table filters

Subscription Management (/owner/subscription)

Plan Status: current tier, usage meters (shifts/month)

Billing Info: credit card form, invoice list

Upgrade/Downgrade: trigger payment flow or Cloud Function

Trial Status: days left, extend trial button

Tests: subscription page render, form validation
