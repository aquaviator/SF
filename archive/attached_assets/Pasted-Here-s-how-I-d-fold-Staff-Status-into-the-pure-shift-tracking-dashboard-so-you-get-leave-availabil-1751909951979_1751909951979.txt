Here’s how I’d fold Staff Status into the pure shift-tracking dashboard—so you get leave & availability alongside your Open/Assigned/Swap/Cancel/Confirmed flow.

1. Quick KPIs (Top Strip)
Widget	Example	What It Tells You
Total Staff	11 (+2 MoM)	Headcount and month-over-month change
Staff Utilisation	90 %	% of staff available (not on leave/sick)
Open Opportunities	40	Shifts broadcast; waiting for pickup
Assigned Shifts	12	Invites sent; awaiting acceptance
Confirmed Shifts	14	Locked-in slots
Swap Requests	5	Staff-initiated shifts to be swapped
Cancel Requests	3	Pending cancellation approvals

Staff Utilisation = (Total Staff – Staff Off Today) / Total Staff. Click it to drill into who’s out and why.

3. Status Definitions & Workflow
Status	Definition	Owner Actions
Open→Opportunity	Shift broadcast to staff pool.	Send targeted invites or auto-offer.
Assigned	Individual has been notified but hasn’t accepted yet.	Check deadlines; remind or reassign as needed.
Swap Pending	Staff A owns it but wants to swap with Staff B.	Review candidates; approve or reassign.
Cancel Pending	Staff A asked to cancel entirely; slot may revert to Open.	Approve cancellation; rebroadcast as Open.
Confirmed	Staff member has accepted; shift is locked in.	Check conflicts; send reminders; mark completed.
